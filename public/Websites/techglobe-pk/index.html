How a corporate quote works

Business buyers request quotes mainly over WhatsApp. Three steps, all in one chat.

  1. 1

    Share the requirement

    Message 0339-0006493 with four things. The builder above writes this message for you.

    • Company name
    • Products
    • Quantities
    • Delivery city
  2. 2

    Receive the quote

    The corporate desk replies in the same chat with options and pricing for your list. Ask questions or change quantities there.

  3. 3

    Delivery to your city

    Once the quote is agreed, the order is arranged for delivery to the city you named.
