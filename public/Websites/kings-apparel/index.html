From yarn to carton, under one roof.

Most brands deal with a separate knitter, dye house, printer and sewing unit. At Kings Apparel these stages sit within one company in Korangi, so a style can move from knitting to warehousing without leaving it.

  1. 1

    Knitting

    Yarn is knitted into fabric on site.

  2. 2

    Dyeing

    Greige fabric is dyed to the shade in your order.

  3. 3

    Printing

    Prints go onto panels or finished pieces.

  4. 4

    Embroidery

    Logos and details are embroidered in house.

  5. 5

    Stitching

    Panels are sewn and finished into garments.

  6. 6

    Warehousing

    Finished goods are packed and held for shipment.
