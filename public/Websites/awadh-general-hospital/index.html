Come in at any hour

The emergency department is open 24/7. If you can, call ahead on 021-35017428 so the team knows you are coming. Bring what you can of:

  • The patient's CNIC, or a family member's
  • The medicines they take, or the empty packets
  • Old prescriptions, reports or discharge papers

دن ہو یا رات، کسی بھی وقت آئیں۔ ہو سکے تو پہلے ۰۲۱-۳۵۰۱۷۴۲۸ پر کال کر دیں۔

Call emergency 021-35017428

See a doctor in the OPD

Outpatient clinics cover endocrinology, ENT, general surgery, paediatrics, dermatology and gynaecology. Call to ask which clinics are running today, or send a visit request and the desk will call you back.

Treatment for your staff, on a company panel

کمپنیوں کے ملازمین کے لیے پینل

We work with companies on panel arrangements — contact our billing office. If you look after HR or admin for a factory or office in Landhi or Korangi, call and ask for billing to talk through what a panel would cover.

ہم کمپنیوں کے ساتھ پینل کے انتظامات کرتے ہیں۔ تفصیل کے لیے بلنگ آفس سے رابطہ کریں۔

Call 021-35017428 and ask for billing

Useful to have ready when you call

  • How many employees the panel would cover
  • Whether family members are included
  • Which services to cover: OPD, admission, lab, emergency
  • How, and how often, you want to be billed
  • A contact person in your HR or admin team
