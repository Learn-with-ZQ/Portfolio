Six commodities, two directions

Each line below says which way it moves and what to put in an enquiry so the desk can answer with a firm offer or bid. Grades, origins and quantities are agreed per contract.

Coming into Pakistan

Milk powderImport

Powdered milk bought abroad in bulk and brought in for buyers in Pakistan's wholesale and food trade.

Useful in an enquiry

  • Whole or skimmed, and fat content
  • Quantity and bag size
  • Delivery point in Pakistan

ClovesImport

Whole dried cloves, imported for the spice wholesale trade that runs through Karachi's markets.

Useful in an enquiry

  • Grade and origin preference
  • Quantity and packing
  • Delivery point in Pakistan

Both ways

Black pepperImport and export

The one commodity on both sides of the board. Noble lists black pepper among its imports and its exports, so a buyer and a seller can both start here.

Useful in an enquiry

  • Whether you are buying or selling
  • Grade or density, and origin
  • Quantity, packing and port

Going out of Pakistan

RiceExport

Rice from Pakistan for overseas buyers, contracted by variety and grade.

Useful in an enquiry

  • Variety, grade and broken percentage
  • Quantity and bag size
  • Destination port

MaizeExport

Maize from Pakistan, for feed, milling and food buyers abroad.

Useful in an enquiry

  • Intended use and moisture limit
  • Quantity and packing, bagged or bulk
  • Destination port

WheatExport

Wheat from Pakistan, offered to overseas buyers against their specification.

Useful in an enquiry

  • Grade and protein, if specified
  • Quantity and packing
  • Destination port

Trading since 1977

Noble began in 1977 by importing tea from Kenya. By its own account, that tea still arrives as part of the routine.

Lines have been added since then. Milk powder and cloves come in. Rice, maize and wheat go out. Black pepper does both. A buyer writing today reaches the same name that took in its first tea consignment in 1977.

Tea has its own house in Jodia Bazar

Tea, the line Noble started with, is handled by its sister concern, Noble Tea & Commodities. It sits in Jodia Bazar, Karachi's old wholesale quarter for tea, spices and grain.
