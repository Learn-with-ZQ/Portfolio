Arabic and Islamic studies, inside the school curriculum

Five subjects published as part of the Reflections curriculum, taught on the same campus as the rest of the school day.

  • ArabicThe language of the Qur'an: reading, writing and understanding it.
  • TajweedThe rules for reciting the Qur'an correctly and beautifully.
  • TafsirExplanation of the meaning of the Qur'an.
  • HadeethThe recorded sayings and practice of the Prophet, peace be upon him.
  • SeerahThe life of the Prophet, peace be upon him.

Hifz

Memorising the Qur'an, one portion at a time. Reflections has described a Hifz programme that begins from class three.

Ask the office how Hifz and regular classes are arranged this session, and how progress is shared with families.
