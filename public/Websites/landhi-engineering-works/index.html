Two product lines, one works

Leaf springs carry the load; radiators keep the engine cool. Both are made at the Landhi works for vehicle makers' lines, fleets and the replacement market.

Leaf spring assemblies

Multi-leaf suspension springs for heavy vehicles, made to the vehicle maker's drawing or matched to a replacement sample.

  • Trucks, buses, trailers and dumpers
  • Complete assemblies and individual leaves
  • OEM supply and replacement market

Radiators

Radiator cores and complete assemblies for automobiles and industrial engines: tanks, tubes and fins built as one cooling unit.

  • Radiator cores and full assemblies
  • Automobiles and industrial engines
  • OEM supply and replacement market
