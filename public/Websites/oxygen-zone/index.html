Equipment on rental and sale basis

Rates are not listed online. Ask about any item below and Oxygen Zone will share the current rental or sale rate on WhatsApp.

  • Oxygen concentrator

    Makes oxygen from room air on mains power, so there is nothing to refill. Commonly used for patients who need oxygen at home over a longer period.

    Ask rental rate

  • Oxygen cylinder

    Stored oxygen that works without electricity. Often kept as a backup for power cuts, or for short-term and on-the-move use.

    Ask rental rate

  • BiPAP / CPAP Breathing support with a mask

    Machines that deliver air pressure through a mask, used for sleep apnoea and breathing support at the settings a doctor prescribes.

    Ask rental rate

  • Medical bed

    An adjustable bed that makes sitting up, turning and day-to-day care easier for a patient recovering or resting at home.

    Ask rental rate

  • Ventilator

    For patients who need mechanical breathing support at home, used under a doctor's direction.

    Ask rental rate

Renting or buying?

Renting

Often chosen for recovery after a hospital stay, or while a doctor is still deciding what long-term care is needed.

Buying

Can make more sense when the need is expected to continue for many months. Ask for both rates and compare.
