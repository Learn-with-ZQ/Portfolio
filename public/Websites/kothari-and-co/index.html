How an export enquiry works

From the first email to the container leaving port, an order moves through four stages.

  1. 1

    Enquiry

    Send the product, approximate quantity, packing and destination port. Email info@kothari.com.pk or call +92-21-32427396.

  2. 2

    Specification and samples

    Grade, size, moisture, packing and labelling are pinned down, with samples where the product calls for them.

  3. 3

    Contract

    Price, quantity, payment terms, shipment window and the documents required are set out in writing.

  4. 4

    Shipment

    Goods are packed to the agreed specification and shipped with the agreed export documents.

A general outline of an export order. Kothari confirms the terms of each order directly.
