One school from the first classroom to college.

Profectus runs from pre-school to college, so a child can move up one stage at a time without changing schools.

  1. 1

    Pre-school

    Where it begins

    Early years classes where children settle into school life through play, stories, early letters and numbers.

  2. 2

    Primary

    The foundations

    Reading, writing, mathematics and science, and the study habits a child carries into every later class.

  3. 3

    Secondary

    Towards examinations

    Subject teaching that builds towards board examinations, in the routes the school offers.

  4. 4

    College

    Before university

    The final years of school, preparing students for university and what comes after.

Cambridge and Matric. The school describes its teaching as Cambridge and Matric education. Ask your campus which routes it offers at each stage.

How admission usually works.

Four steps from your first question to your child's first day. Typical process; confirm the exact steps with the school.

  1. 1

    Inquiry

    Call 0340 4190000 or send us the form below. Tell us your child's class and the campus you prefer.

  2. 2

    Campus visit

    Walk round the campus, see the classrooms and ask the admissions team your questions.

  3. 3

    Assessment

    Your child sits an assessment for the class applied for. The school confirms the date and time.

  4. 4

    Enrolment

    Once a place is offered, complete the forms and fee formalities at the campus office.

Session 2026-27

Applications for the 2026-27 session opened in August 2026, with an open house at all campuses on 8 August and assessment timings posted on 10 August.

Those dates have passed. Call the school to ask about places, the next visit day and upcoming assessments.
