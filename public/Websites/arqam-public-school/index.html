Faith and learning in the same school day

At Arqam, Islamic values are not a separate subject squeezed in at the end of the week. They shape how children learn, speak and treat each other, alongside a full academic timetable.

Sample content — programme details to be supplied by Arqam Public School.

Values

Children learn their deen as part of everyday school life: in the way a lesson begins, how classmates are spoken to, and how teachers are respected.

  • Qur'an and Islamic studies as part of the weekly timetable
  • Adab and good character expected in class, in the corridor and at home
  • Respect for parents, teachers and one another

Academics

A steady, structured school day: reading and number work in the early years, then the full set of subjects children need for the board years ahead.

  • Early years: reading, writing and number sense
  • Primary and middle: English, Urdu, mathematics, science, Islamiat and social studies
  • Senior classes: preparation for board examinations

Fees, results and notices in one parent app

The school's site already links a Student Portal app on Google Play. This concept proposes one parent portal connected to every campus, so a family with children at two campuses sees everything in one login.

  • Fee vouchersMonthly vouchers for each child, with what is paid and what is due
  • AttendanceDaily attendance, with a message to the parent on an absence
  • ResultsTerm reports and teacher remarks as soon as they are issued
  • NoticesHolidays, events and campus news from the right campus only
