From tech pack to packed carton

In a CMT order the buyer owns the design and the materials. The factory's job is the making. This is how a style travels along the line.

  1. You send

    • Approved tech pack and measurements
    • Fabric
    • Trims and labels
    • Packing instructions
  2. 1

    Cutting

    Fabric is spread, marked and cut to the approved pattern, then bundled by size.

  3. 2

    Stitching

    Bundles move along the sewing lines and are assembled to the construction notes in your tech pack.

  4. 3

    Finishing

    Loose threads are trimmed, garments pressed, and labels and trims attached.

  5. 4

    Checking

    Pieces are measured and inspected against your spec sheet and the approved sample.

  6. 5

    Packing

    Folded, tagged and packed by size ratio and carton instruction.

  7. You receive

    • Finished garments
    • Checked against your spec
    • Packed to your instructions
