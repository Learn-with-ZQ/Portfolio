What we make

Four families of corrugated packaging, each designed and tested around the product it has to carry.

  • Shipping containers

    Regular slotted cartons and custom shippers, sized to your product, your pallet and the way it travels.

  • Retail packaging

    Printed corrugated packs that leave the warehouse and go straight onto the shelf.

  • Stock boxes

    Standard sizes for everyday packing, storage and repeat orders.

  • In-store displays

    Counter units, floor stands and shelf-ready trays for launches and promotions.

M&F also lists rotogravure printing among its specialisms, and names a separate M&F Maymar Flexible Factory.

Corrugated board in section: a fluted medium bonded between two liners.

How a box moves through the factory

From paper reel to loaded pallet, a typical corrugated order moves through these stages, on the machines the company names on its own site. Not every job needs every step.

  1. 1

    Corrugate

    112″ corrugator

    Paper is fluted and bonded between liners to make board, then cut to sheet sizes for your job.

  2. 2

    Print

    Flexo printing, in line on the folder gluers

    Your artwork and colours go onto the board before it is shaped.

  3. 3

    Die-cut

    Five rotary diecutters

    Shaped styles, windows, handholds and display parts are cut and creased.

  4. 4

    Fold & glue

    Six flexo folder gluers

    Blanks are folded and the glue flap joined, so boxes arrive flat and open square on your line.

  5. 5

    Dispatch

    Warehousing and distribution

    Finished boxes are bundled, stored and delivered to your warehouse.

Services listed by M&F: design, testing, project management, manufacturing, warehousing and distribution.
