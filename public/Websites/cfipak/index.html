Service on file

Sea freight

Sea cargo in and out of Karachi, booked by CFI as an NVOCC (a non-vessel operating common carrier), with the clearance kept on the same file.

Put these in your enquiry

  • Port of loading and port of discharge
  • Full container or part load, and container size
  • Commodity, gross weight and volume
Enquire about sea freight

Shipping containers and general logistics are on the service list too. Ask about either in the enquiry.

One job file, from enquiry to delivery

Freight and customs clearance can mean two firms and two sets of papers. CFI offers both, so one enquiry can carry the booking, the customs entry and the delivery of the same shipment.

Job file enquiry to delivery

  1. 1 Open

    Enquiry

    You send the mode, route, cargo and weight. The enquiry form on this page writes it up for you.

    Typically on fileEnquiry note
    Commercial invoice draft

  2. 2 Book

    Booking

    Space is booked by air, sea or rail, and the shipping documents are raised against the same file.

    Typically on fileBooking confirmation
    Bill of lading or air waybill

  3. 3 Clear

    Clearance

    The goods declaration is filed and the cargo is cleared through customs at the port or airport.

    Typically on fileGoods declaration
    Duty and tax assessment

  4. 4 Close

    Delivery

    Cargo goes on to the consignee or into warehousing, and the file closes with the delivery papers.

    Typically on fileDelivery order
    Proof of delivery

Document names are the usual ones for Pakistani imports and exports; the exact set depends on the cargo and the route.
