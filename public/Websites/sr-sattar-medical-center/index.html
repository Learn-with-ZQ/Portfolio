When the patient can't come to Korangi No. 1

The centre lists home healthcare among its services. Tell them who needs care, where, and when, and they will tell you what they can arrange.

  • Doctor or physio visit

    A doctor or physiotherapist comes to the home, for patients who find the trip difficult or are recovering in bed.

  • Sample collection at home

    Blood and other samples taken at home for the centre's laboratory, so the patient doesn't wait in a queue.

  • Elderly care

    Care at home for older family members. Describe what is needed and the centre will say how it can help.
