A neighbourhood hospital, on record

Mamji Hospital was established in 1998 and has 85 beds.1 Its public profile lists 114 doctors across around 30 specialties, including cardiology, neurosurgery and oncology, with emergency care around the clock.1 The Sindh Healthcare Commission lists it as Mamji Hospital Orthopaedic & General under provisional licence SHCC/DLA/PL/0217.2

Before a planned admission

  1. Call your insurer or HR desk to confirm Mamji Hospital is on your current panel.
  2. Bring your insurance card, CNIC and the doctor's admission advice.
  3. Visit the hospital's billing desk on arrival so the claim can be opened.
