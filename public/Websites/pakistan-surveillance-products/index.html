CCTV supply for the people who install it.

Pakistan Surveillance Products distributes video surveillance products to dealers, installers and system integrators, from a showroom in the CCTV and Solar Market at Zamzama Centre, Saddar. In business since 2010.

A supplier, not a shop front

The business sells to the trade. Whichever part of it you work in, the showroom is set up to supply the job, not a single home.

Dealers

Restocking your own counter. Ask for the current models, quantities on hand and trade terms.

Installers

Fitting out a site this week. Collect the cameras, the recorder and the cabling for the job in one visit to Saddar.

System integrators

Planning a larger system. Bring the drawing or the camera count and talk through models for each area.
