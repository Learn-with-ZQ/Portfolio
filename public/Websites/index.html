How these were made

  • Each business was researched first: public listings, trade portals, association directories and any existing website. Only verified details (addresses, phone numbers, products, years) appear as fact.
  • Where a business has no website or brand, the wordmark and palette are a proposed identity. Existing brand colours were evolved, not replaced.
  • Nothing is invented: no testimonials, ratings, awards, prices or statistics. Missing content is marked “to be supplied” or “sample”.
  • Every page is a single responsive HTML file with no backend. Forms compose a WhatsApp, SMS or email message, or say plainly that they are a demo.
