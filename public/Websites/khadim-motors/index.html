Five kinds of part, matched at the counter

The range sits in five groups. Most parts are matched by the vehicle and engine, or by the number and sizes stamped on the old part, so bring either when you ask.

  • Piston rings

    Compression and oil-control rings that seal the piston against the cylinder wall. Ask by engine model and size, standard or oversize.

  • Brake pads & shoes

    Disc pads and drum shoes. Bring the old part, or the vehicle model and year, so the counter can match the shape.

  • Oil seals

    Crankshaft, camshaft, hub and gearbox seals. Seals are matched by size, so note the inner diameter, outer diameter and width printed on the old one.

  • Cylinders

    Wheel, master and clutch cylinders are the usual requests. Say which one, and the vehicle it comes from.

  • Timing & fan belts

    Timing belts are matched by tooth count and width; fan belts by length and profile. The code printed on the back of the old belt is the quickest route.
