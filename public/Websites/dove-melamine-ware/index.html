Looking after melamine

General guidance for melamine dinnerware and tempered-glass lids. Follow the instructions supplied with your Dove set.

Do

  • Wash with warm, soapy water and a soft sponge.
  • Serve hot and cold food straight to the table.
  • Let glass lids cool before washing them.
  • Stack plates dry, with like sizes together.

Avoid

  • Microwave and oven use; most melamine is not made for either.
  • Steel wool and scouring powder, which dull the gloss.
  • Plunging a hot glass lid into cold water.
  • Cutting directly on the plate with a sharp knife.
