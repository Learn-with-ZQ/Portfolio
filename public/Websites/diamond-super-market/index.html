How ordering online works

Three steps from basket to doorstep. The delivery charge is shown at checkout.

  1. 1

    Fill your basket

    Choose your area on dsmonline.pk, add what you need, and check out with your address and phone number.

  2. 2

    Take our call

    Our customer service team phones the number on your order to confirm it before it is processed. Keep your phone close.

  3. 3

    Receive your groceries

    Your confirmed order is delivered to your door. Check the items when they arrive.
