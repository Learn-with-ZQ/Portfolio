Epoxy flooring

A seamless resin floor laid over prepared concrete. It cures hard and glossy, wipes clean, and can be broadcast with coloured flakes or quartz for grip.

Finish
Gloss or satin, plain or flake
Where it goes
Warehouses, workshops, showrooms, car parks

PU flooring

Polyurethane systems stay slightly more flexible than epoxy, so they cope better with hot wash-downs, temperature swings and spills.

Finish
Satin or matt, smooth or textured
Where it goes
Food processing, kitchens, labs, cold rooms
