Five parts of the driver's cabin

Each family is made for a cab's own dimensions and fixing points, for vehicle-maker programmes and for replacement in the aftermarket.

  • SunvisorsOverhead, front

    Driver and passenger visors shaped to the roof line and windscreen of each cab.

    Ask about sunvisors

  • Roof headliningOverhead

    The lining across the inside of the roof, finishing the cabin overhead and around its fittings.

    Ask about headlining

  • Door trimInside each door

    Inner door panels that cover the door structure and carry the armrest, handle and pocket.

    Ask about door trim

  • Floor matsCab floor

    Mats cut to the cab floor, around the pedals, gear lever and seat mounts.

    Ask about floor mats

  • Side mirrorsCab exterior

    Exterior mirrors and housings mounted on the cab for the driver's view behind.

    Ask about side mirrors
