A Karachi towel maker for more than five decades.

Shaheen Industries was established in 1973 to manufacture terry towels and allied home furnishing products. It still works from its own integrated manufacturing unit in Karachi, where the weaving, dyeing and finishing teams each check the cloth as it passes through their hands.

Production is for export, mainly to buyers in Europe and the USA, and every order is made to the customer's own specification, from the choice of yarn to the dyeing.

Checked three times, the last against your standard.

The company describes its quality assurance in three layers, from the loom to the final inspection.

  1. 1

    In-line checks

    The weaving, dyeing and finishing teams check the work at their own stage, as it moves through the unit.

  2. 2

    A separate QC team

    A quality control team, apart from production, works under the daily supervision of the Quality Manager.

  3. 3

    Final inspection

    Finished goods are inspected against the buyer's own quality standard.

Outside testing. Products are tested regularly at an outside standard laboratory.

OEKO-TEX. Dyeing to OEKO-TEX is done when the customer asks for it.
