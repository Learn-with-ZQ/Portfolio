Mr. Hatim H. Gabaji started Meditec Instruments after 22 years at a multinational trading company working in medical and diagnostic products. The company was incorporated in Karachi on 12 October 1989.

Today it is run by his daughter, Tasneem, and his son-in-law, Hozaifa Hashim, with the company's sales and technical staff.
