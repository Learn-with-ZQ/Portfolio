Rice and eggs for the table. Feed and vaccine for the farm.

Niraav Foods Pakistan is a venture of M. Wali Group, with a background in the human food and animal feed sector. Three divisions, one head office in Korangi Industrial Area and a factory at Port Qasim, Karachi.
